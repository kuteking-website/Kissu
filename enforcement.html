Automated Safety & AI-Assisted Detection

Kissu uses automated and AI-assisted safety systems to help identify potential violations of its Acceptable Use Policy and platform rules.

Depending on the feature and information available, these systems may analyze relevant profile information, user-generated content, account activity, behavioral signals, and reports for indicators of prohibited or suspicious activity.

Potential violations may include:

  • Fraud and scam-related behavior;
  • Impersonation or suspicious profiles;
  • Harassment, threats, or abusive behavior;
  • Prohibited sexual or exploitative content;
  • Spam or suspicious account activity;
  • Other activity that may violate Kissu's Acceptable Use Policy.

Automated signals may trigger additional review, restrictions, or other safety actions depending on the nature and severity of the detected risk.

Kissu combines automated detection with user reporting, blocking controls, administrative moderation tools, and enforcement procedures to help prevent and respond to prohibited activity.
