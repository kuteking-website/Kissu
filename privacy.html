Kissu Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kuteking Global, LLC (“Kissu,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, processes, stores, shares, and protects information when you use Kissu, including the Kissu Telegram bot, Telegram Mini App, website, built-in chat, Stories, Private Lounge features, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Kissu is an online dating and social matching service intended only for adults who are at least 18 years old and meet the applicable legal age requirements in their jurisdiction.

By using Kissu, you acknowledge the information practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Kissu and which features you use.

A. Account and Telegram Information

When you access Kissu through Telegram, we may receive information made available through Telegram, including:

  • Telegram user ID;
  • Telegram username;
  • First and last name, where available;
  • Profile information made available to Kissu;
  • Language or other account-related information made available through Telegram; and
  • Information necessary to authenticate and maintain your Kissu account.

Telegram may independently collect and process information according to its own privacy practices.

B. Dating Profile Information

When you create or update your Kissu profile, we may collect information including:

  • Name or display name;
  • Age or date-of-birth-related information;
  • Gender;
  • Dating and discovery preferences;
  • Location or location information you provide;
  • Profile photographs and other uploaded media;
  • Biography or “About Me” information;
  • Interests;
  • Relationship preferences;
  • Other information you voluntarily provide through your profile.

Because Kissu is a dating service, some information you voluntarily provide may reveal or relate to sensitive aspects of your personal life, relationships, preferences, or identity.

You should only provide information that you are comfortable sharing with Kissu and, where applicable, other users.

C. Discovery, Likes and Matching Information

When you use Kissu's discovery and matching features, we may collect or generate information including:

  • Profiles shown to you;
  • Swipes, likes, passes, and similar actions;
  • Users who express interest in you;
  • Matches;
  • Search and discovery preferences;
  • Match and interaction history;
  • Profile visibility and boost activity; and
  • Other signals generated through your use of Kissu.

We may use this information to operate, personalize, evaluate, and improve Kissu's discovery and matching features.

D. Messages and Communications

When you use Kissu's built-in chat or other Kissu-operated communication features, we process information necessary to provide and protect those services.

This may include:

  • Messages you send and receive;
  • Sender and recipient information;
  • Conversation and match identifiers;
  • Message timestamps;
  • Delivery and interaction information where available; and
  • Reports, blocks, or safety actions associated with conversations.

Kissu may process communications where reasonably necessary to provide the messaging service, operate safety and moderation systems, investigate reports, enforce our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy, prevent fraud or abuse, protect users, or comply with legal obligations.

E. Stories and User-Generated Content

When you use Kissu Stories or other content-sharing features, we may process:

  • Photos, videos, text, and other submitted content;
  • Story views;
  • Likes, reactions, or expressions of interest;
  • Interaction timestamps;
  • Resulting matches or conversations; and
  • Safety or moderation information associated with that content.

Content you intentionally publish through discoverable features may be visible to other Kissu users according to the functionality and settings of those features.

F. Private Lounge Information

Kissu may allow eligible matched users to access an optional Private Lounge through Telegram.

Private Lounges use Telegram functionality and are distinct from Kissu's built-in chat.

Kissu may process information necessary to create, associate, manage, secure, or record the status of a Private Lounge, including:

  • Users associated with the Lounge;
  • Match and Lounge identifiers;
  • Creation and status information;
  • Reports or blocks initiated through Kissu;
  • Safety and enforcement actions; and
  • Information reasonably necessary to administer the feature.

Information processed independently by Telegram in connection with Private Lounges is subject to Telegram's own privacy practices.

Users should exercise caution before voluntarily sharing phone numbers, usernames, addresses, financial information, or other personal information with another user.

G. Payments, Gifts and Subscription Information

When you purchase, send, receive, or use Kissu VIP, digital gifts, boosts, or other premium features, we may collect or process:

  • Transaction identifiers;
  • Product or subscription purchased;
  • Purchase amount or digital currency amount;
  • Payment or transaction status;
  • Subscription status and duration;
  • Gift sender and recipient information where applicable;
  • Boost activity;
  • Payment confirmation; and
  • Transaction history.

Payments may be processed through Telegram Stars or other authorized third-party payment providers, depending on where and how a transaction occurs.

Kissu does not store complete payment-card numbers when payment credentials are handled directly by third-party payment providers.

If you access Kissu through a referral, advertising campaign, creator, affiliate, or promotional link, we may collect information including:

  • Referral identifiers;
  • Campaign identifiers;
  • Referral source;
  • Affiliate or creator attribution;
  • Registration and conversion events;
  • Activity associated with the referral or campaign; and
  • Purchase attribution where applicable.
